About TechRevive

A Repair Service Built Around Your Life

TechRevive Wireless Repairs started in Smithfield, NC with one goal: make professional phone repair genuinely convenient. Not "book an appointment and drive to the mall" convenient — we come to you convenient.

Every repair is handled by Chris, a certified technician with hands-on experience across hundreds of devices. Whether it's a cracked iPhone screen, a dead battery, or water damage that happened 20 minutes ago, TechRevive responds fast and gets it fixed right.

We've fixed phones in parking lots at 11pm, driven an hour to reach customers at work, and mounted TVs on the same visit as a phone repair. That's the kind of service we believe every Johnston County resident deserves.

Our Values

Transparency First

Free diagnostics, upfront quotes, and no surprise charges. You always know the price before we start. If we can't fix it, you pay nothing.

Community Over Convenience

TechRevive is local. Every repair keeps money in Johnston County, not in a corporate chain. We care about our community because we live in it.

Quality That Lasts

We only use premium replacement parts and back every repair with a warranty. Fast work doesn't mean cutting corners — it means being efficient and skilled.

Availability When It Matters

Phone emergencies don't happen on a schedule. We're available evenings, weekends, and yes — late on Friday nights when you really need help.

Genuine Expertise

Your Time Is Valuable
