Liquid Damage

Water Damage Repair

Free Diagnostic

Dropped it in water? Don't put it in rice — call us immediately. The faster we treat liquid damage, the better the odds of a full recovery. We open, dry, treat corrosion, and test every component on-site.

  • Immediate response service available
  • Internal drying and corrosion treatment
  • Component-by-component testing
  • Works on pool, toilet, rain, and drink spills
  • Free assessment — you only pay if we fix it
04
Charging Issues

Charging Port Repair

Starting at $55

If your phone won't charge or only charges at certain angles, the charging port is usually the culprit. Lint, debris, and wear all degrade ports over time. We clean or replace the port and get you powered up fast.

  • Lightning, USB-C, and Micro-USB ports
  • Deep cleaning before replacement (often solves it)
  • Full charging test post-repair
  • Covers all major brands
  • 90-day warranty

Camera Repair

Starting at $65

Blurry photos, cracked lens covers, autofocus failures, or a camera that won't open at all — we diagnose the root cause and replace only what needs replacing. Front and rear camera repairs available.

  • Lens cover replacement (most affordable)
  • Full camera module replacement
  • Front-facing and rear camera
  • Video and photo quality testing
  • All iPhone and Samsung models

Speaker & Microphone Repair

  • Microphone replacement
  • Audio testing with multiple scenarios
  • All major brands supported
